# HPC-02 — Curiosity

## Definition

Curiosity is a family of motivational states/processes that promote exploration and information seeking. There is no single universally accepted definition or mechanism.

## Underlying mechanisms

Research supports several partially overlapping drivers:

- novelty
- uncertainty
- expected information gain
- learning progress
- subjective value of information

Recent neuroscience work suggests that information itself can carry subjective value and recruit value-based decision mechanisms.

The classic "information gap" idea is useful, but it should not be treated as the only mechanism of curiosity.

## Evidence

Reviews in psychology and neuroscience consistently treat curiosity as a driver of information-seeking and exploration.

A 2024 review describes uncertainty, information gain, and learning progress as interacting drivers rather than one universal trigger.

## Boundary conditions

- Curiosity is multidimensional.
- More uncertainty does not always mean more curiosity.
- People may avoid information when it is threatening, costly, irrelevant, or conflicts with other goals.
- Novelty and surprise can trigger exploration without necessarily producing sustained curiosity.

## Social-media relevance

Curiosity helps explain:
- opening a post;
- continuing a sequence;
- searching a creator/profile;
- seeking missing context;
- exploring a topic beyond the first exposure.

But "curiosity gap" should not be reduced to deceptive withholding.

## Core connections

- **Curiosity -> Attention**: information-seeking directs attention toward expected information. **Strongly supported.**
- **Curiosity <-> Valuation**: information can acquire subjective value; curiosity can be modeled partly through value-based choice. **Strongly supported / evolving theory.**
- **Curiosity -> Reinforcement**: satisfying information seeking may support repeated exploration, but direct social-media habit formation requires more evidence. **Emerging.**
- **Trust -> Curiosity pursuit**: trusted sources may lower the cost/risk of seeking further information, but this edge remains **Hypothesis** until directly verified.
