# HPC-06 — Connection

## Definition

Connection is the perceived relational bond between audience and creator/source. In creator contexts, an important form is parasocial interaction/relationship: psychologically meaningful engagement that can exist without symmetrical real-world reciprocity.

## Underlying mechanisms

Research commonly describes parasocial engagement using combinations of:

- cognitive processing
- affect / emotional involvement
- perceived similarity or homophily
- familiarity
- repeated exposure
- behavioral engagement

The construct should not be simplified to "people think the creator is their real friend."

## Evidence

A mixed-methods study integrating a meta-analysis of 117 studies (47,647 respondents) found parasocial engagement related to brand associations and purchase intentions and identified creator characteristics such as expertise, trustworthiness, attractiveness, and congruence as important elements.

A 2026 systematic review synthesized 76 peer-reviewed studies on parasocial interaction/relationships with online influencers, reinforcing that antecedents, consequences, mediators, and moderators vary substantially.

## Boundary conditions

- Parasocial engagement can have beneficial, neutral, or harmful consequences depending on outcome and context.
- Connection is not equivalent to trust.
- Repeated exposure alone is insufficient to guarantee attachment.
- Age, culture, content type, and platform may moderate effects.

## Social-media relevance

Connection helps explain:
- creator loyalty;
- emotional investment;
- perceived closeness;
- willingness to keep following;
- greater receptivity to creator communication.

## Core connections

- **Connection <-> Trust**: related but distinct; relational attachment and source evaluation can reinforce one another. **Moderately supported.**
- **Identity -> Connection**: homophily/congruence can support perceived affinity. **Moderately supported.**
- **Connection -> Reinforcement/Return behavior**: plausible because relational value can motivate repeated engagement, but direct habit mechanism remains **Emerging**.
- **Connection -> Social Transmission**: loyalty/affiliation can motivate advocacy, but a general causal edge requires stronger direct synthesis. **Emerging.**
