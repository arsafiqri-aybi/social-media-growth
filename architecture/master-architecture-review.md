# Social Media Growth — Master Architecture Review v0.1

Date: 2026-10-05  
Status: **PROVISIONAL ARCHITECTURE LOCK**  
Scope: Global Social Media Growth Intelligence System, preserving the accepted Human Psychological Core (HPC) baseline.

## 1. Executive decision

The existing HPC is retained as the **human psychological foundation**, not expanded into the whole social-media-growth ontology.

After state recovery, literature review, platform-mechanism review, and adversarial decomposition, the current best global architecture is:

```text
GOVERNANCE
objectives · constraints · resources · ethics · horizon
        │
        ▼
CONTEXT / ENVIRONMENT  ↔  all layers
platform · culture · audience state · creator state · category · time
        │
        ▼
HUMAN PSYCHOLOGICAL FOUNDATION
HPC: attention · curiosity · valuation/emotion · identity · trust
     connection · social transmission · reinforcement/habit
        │
        ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Audience & Value Intelligence                                  │
│ 2. Communication & Content Intelligence                           │
│ 3. Distribution & Discovery Intelligence                          │
│ 4. Relationship & Retention Intelligence                          │
│ 5. Learning & Adaptation Intelligence                             │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        ↕ many-to-many typed connections
OBSERVATION → DIAGNOSIS → INTERVENTION → OUTCOME → UPDATE
```

The five operational cores are accepted **provisionally** as the smallest currently defensible decomposition that:
- covers the recurring domains found in broad social-media research;
- preserves distinct causal and operational failure modes;
- avoids promoting every important topic into its own core;
- supports account-level diagnosis, measurement, experimentation, and feedback.

This is not a claim that exactly five cores are a scientific law. The structure remains falsifiable.

## 3. Why HPC alone is insufficient

HPC explains human-level mechanisms such as why people notice, seek, value, trust, connect, transmit, and return.

It does not by itself fully represent:
- who the target audience is and what they value;
- how an account chooses positioning and message architecture;
- how value becomes content and creative execution;
- how platform retrieval/ranking/discovery changes exposure;
- how account-level relationship and retention states accumulate over time;
- how observations are converted into experiments and strategy updates;
- how goals, resources, constraints, and platform context change the decision problem.

Therefore HPC is necessary but not sufficient for a full social-media-growth intelligence system.

## 4. Candidate architecture challenge

### 4.1 Candidate: Brand as a separate core
**Decision: reject as a top-level core.**

Reason:
Brand, positioning, identity expression, consistency, source credibility, and memory operate across Communication & Content and Relationship & Retention. Promoting Brand to a separate root creates duplicated mechanisms and unclear boundaries.

Retain brand-related constructs as cross-core subsystems and edges.

### 4.2 Candidate: Algorithm as a separate core
**Decision: reject as a top-level core.**

Reason:
Recommendation, ranking, candidate generation, search, and eligibility are mechanisms of Distribution & Discovery. "Algorithm" is also platform-specific and time-varying, which makes it unsuitable as a timeless root ontology.

Represent platform ranking systems as environment-aware distribution mechanisms.

### 4.3 Candidate: Community as a separate core
**Decision: do not promote.**

Community has real explanatory importance, but current evidence supports representing it as a subsystem at the interface of:
- Relationship & Retention;
- Distribution & Discovery;
- HPC Identity, Connection, Social Transmission, and Reinforcement.

Promoting it to a root would duplicate these mechanisms.

### 4.4 Candidate: Monetization / Conversion as a separate core
**Decision: reject as a universal growth core.**

Conversion and monetization are objectives/outcomes that vary by account mission. A noncommercial education account, community account, creator account, and commerce account can all grow without sharing the same conversion objective.

Represent commercial conversion under Governance objective functions and outcome schemas.

### 4.5 Candidate: Production / Operations as a separate core
**Decision: do not promote yet.**

Creator capacity, workflow, production quality, cadence capacity, budget, team structure, and tooling materially affect execution. However they currently fit better as:
- Governance resources/constraints;
- Communication & Content execution capability;
- Context modifiers.

Promote only if future evidence shows an irreducible causal role not expressible through those classes.

### 4.6 Candidate: Strategy as a separate core
**Decision: model above the operational cores.**

Goals, resources, policies, trade-offs, and optimization targets shape the whole system. They are better represented in Governance rather than as another peer core.

### 4.7 Candidate: Context / Environment as a sixth core
**Decision: represent as cross-cutting layer, not peer core.**

Platform, culture, language, category, audience maturity, creator history, temporal state, competitive environment, and affordances can change the strength or meaning of mechanisms across all cores.

They are modifiers and state variables rather than one coherent causal core.

## 5. Five operational cores

### Core 1 — Audience & Value Intelligence

Core question:
**Who are the relevant humans, in what context, and what do they perceive as valuable or relevant?**

Primary responsibilities:
- audience discovery;
- segmentation and heterogeneity;
- goals, needs, jobs, pains, desires;
- identity and cultural context;
- category knowledge and alternatives;
- audience–value fit;
- perceived relevance;
- value hypotheses.

Boundary:
Does not own the psychological mechanism of valuation itself; that remains in HPC. This core models the external/account-side representation of whom value is being created for and what value proposition is being tested.

### Core 2 — Communication & Content Intelligence

Core question:
**How is value encoded into a message/creative object that can be selected, understood, experienced, remembered, and acted on?**

Primary responsibilities:
- positioning;
- message architecture;
- framing;
- copy;
- storytelling;
- visual/audio communication;
- content formats;
- hooks and information scent;
- pacing and structure;
- creative execution;
- calls to action;
- content portfolio design.

Boundary:
Does not own human attention, trust, curiosity, or emotion as psychological constructs. It creates stimuli intended to activate or interact with those HPC mechanisms.

### Core 3 — Distribution & Discovery Intelligence

Core question:
**How does content become eligible, retrieved, ranked, exposed, searched, shared, and discovered by relevant people?**

Primary responsibilities:
- recommendation systems;
- candidate generation/retrieval;
- ranking;
- search/discovery surfaces;
- platform eligibility;
- graph distribution;
- human-to-human diffusion;
- sharing/reposting/collaboration;
- cross-platform pathways.

Boundary:
Distinguish distribution opportunity from observed psychological response. Exposure does not imply attention; recommendation does not imply satisfaction; sharing opportunity does not imply transmission.

### Core 4 — Relationship & Retention Intelligence

Core question:
**How does repeated exposure and interaction become durable relationship, return, participation, loyalty, and advocacy?**

Primary responsibilities:
- credibility accumulation;
- familiarity;
- creator–audience relationship;
- parasocial mechanisms where relevant;
- community participation;
- belonging;
- expectation formation;
- recurring formats/rituals;
- return and retention;
- loyalty and advocacy.

Boundary:
Observed returning behavior is not automatically habit. Trust, connection, identity, and reinforcement mechanisms remain grounded in HPC; this core models their account-level relational accumulation and consequences over time.

### Core 5 — Learning & Adaptation Intelligence

Core question:
**How does the account learn from observed outcomes without confusing metrics with latent mechanisms?**

Primary responsibilities:
- measurement;
- proxy validity;
- analytics interpretation;
- competing explanations;
- experimentation;
- causal inference where possible;
- hypothesis tracking;
- benchmarking;
- prediction vs outcome;
- strategy update;
- calibration.

Boundary:
This core does not rewrite scientific truth from a single account outcome. Account-local evidence updates operational beliefs while scientific evidence remains separately versioned.

## 6. Governance layer

Governance defines:
- account mission;
- target outcome;
- optimization function;
- constraints;
- resources;
- ethical boundaries;
- commercial/noncommercial intent;
- risk tolerance;
- time horizon.

Examples:
- maximize qualified discovery without sacrificing trust;
- grow a community around a specific identity;
- increase product demand;
- build long-term creator authority;
- maximize educational reach while preserving accuracy.

There is no meaningful "growth" without an explicit objective function.

## 7. Context / Environment layer

First-class context dimensions should include at minimum:
- platform;
- content format;
- audience segment;
- culture/language;
- account maturity;
- creator/source state;
- category/topic;
- relationship history;
- temporal state;
- commercial intent;
- resource constraints;
- platform affordances/policies.

Context values qualify mechanisms and evidence; they are not universal multipliers unless empirically calibrated.

## 8. Global ontology rule

Use hierarchy for retrieval/composition, graph edges for causal/functional interaction.

Global levels:

- G0 Governance
- G1 Foundation
- G2 Operational Core
- G3 Subsystem
- G4 Mechanism
- G5 Atomic reasoning unit / neuron
- G6 Typed connection
- G7 Evidence
- G8 Observation / measurement
- G9 Reasoning / decision
- G10 Feedback / learning

Existing HPC L0–L3 remains valid **inside the HPC foundation namespace**. Do not renumber legacy HPC objects destructively. Global hierarchy wraps, rather than rewrites, the local HPC hierarchy.

## 9. Evidence and measurement rules

Preserve existing HPC scientific discipline globally:
- correlation is not causation;
- platform analytics are not direct mind-state measurements;
- account-local calibration does not rewrite scientific evidence;
- effect sizes do not become runtime propagation coefficients automatically;
- uncertainty stays multidimensional;
- negative evidence and contradictions remain visible;
- context transport limits are explicit.

For every latent construct, prefer:
construct → possible behavioral signal → observable metric → ambiguity → competing explanations.
