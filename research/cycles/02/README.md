# R0 + R1 — Cycle 02

Status: **COMPLETE FOR SECOND PASS; NOT SATURATED**

## Main ontology decision

The architecture keeps **8 L0 core anchors**.

This does **not** mean all eight are unitary latent constructs.

Two cores are now explicitly composite:

- **Valuation & Emotion** = valuation subsystem + emotion/affect subsystem.
- **Reinforcement & Habit** = reinforcement/value-updating subsystem + habit/context-response subsystem.

Social Transmission is explicitly a **behavioral-output family**; motives are represented separately.

The 10X runtime must reason at L1/L2. L0 rollups are summaries, not psychometric diagnoses.

## Measurement decision

Every core now has a first-pass measurement-validity map.

High-value restrictions include:

- ANT attention components have uneven reliability.
- CEI-II/5DCR capture different curiosity facets.
- Affect measures do not directly measure subjective value.
- Identity measures are multidimensional and culturally non-invariant in some parameters.
- Perceived credibility is not factual accuracy.
- PSI is not PSR.
- sharing intention is not actual sharing.
- SRBAI measures automaticity, not habit as a whole.

## Non-redundancy audit

Cycle 01 contained **50**, not 51, candidate modules.

Cycle 02 reduces/refactors that to **45 active candidate modules**.

Key moves:

- merge arousal into a broader dimensional-affect L1 module;
- move identity signaling to Social Transmission;
- move homophily to a cross-core similarity connector;
- move reciprocity to relationship context/boundary;
- move audience/channel/device effects to context;
- merge prediction error/value updating into reinforcement-learning module;
- add social bonding/relationship maintenance as a missing transmission motive.
