# R0 + R1 — Cycle 01

Status: **FIRST-PASS COMPLETE; NOT SATURATED**

This cycle touched all eight candidate cores before any deep-dive pass.

## High-value findings

- Attention remains viable as a core, but must expand beyond capture into sustained attention and suppression/disengagement.
- Curiosity remains viable, but information-gap-only models are insufficient; avoidance/cost must be representable.
- **Valuation & Emotion has high split risk.** Keep the umbrella only provisionally.
- Identity remains viable; similarity/homophily must not be equated with identity.
- Trust remains distinct, but perceived credibility and heuristic credentials are not calibrated truth.
- Connection remains viable only if PSI, PSR, familiarity, attachment and reciprocity are separated.
- Social Transmission must separate observed transmission behavior, motives, and platform/network amplification.
- **Reinforcement & Habit has high split risk.** Reinforcement learning and habitual control require separate modules.

## Contradiction discovered

The 2012 emotion/virality literature supports a bounded arousal/transmission link, but two preregistered 2024 replications found no effect of incidental physiological arousal on social-media sharing willingness.

Therefore the architecture must not encode a universal `arousal -> sharing` law.
