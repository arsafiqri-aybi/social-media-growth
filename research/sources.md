# Core Evidence Registry v0.1

This is not a complete bibliography. It records sources currently carrying important architectural claims.

## Attention / value

1. Anderson BA. **Neurobiology of value-driven attention.** Current Opinion in Psychology. 2019.
   - Supports reward-learning effects on attentional priority.
   - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.copsyc.2018.11.004

2. Stanković M. **Systematic review of the value-driven attentional capture paradigm in visual attention studies: Evidence from 52 experiments.** Acta Psychologica. 2025.
   - Supports reward-associated attentional capture while documenting methodological concerns and weak effects in the traditional paradigm.
   - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2025.105060

3. Anderson BA, Kim H. **On the relationship between value-driven and stimulus-driven attentional capture.** Attention, Perception, & Psychophysics. 2019.
   - Distinguishes reward history, salience, and goal relevance.
   - https://doi.org/10.3758/s13414-019-01670-2

## Curiosity / information seeking

4. Gruber MJ, Ranganath C. **The Psychology and Neuroscience of Curiosity.** Neuron. 2015.
   - Curiosity as a basic cognitive/motivational phenomenon with unresolved definitional questions.
   - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2015.09.010

5. Gottlieb J, Oudeyer PY, Lopes M, Baranes A. **Information-seeking, curiosity, and attention: computational and neural mechanisms.** Trends in Cognitive Sciences. 2013.
   - Connects novelty, surprise, learning progress, information gain, attention, and exploration.
   - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.001

6. Kobayashi K, Kable JW. **Neural mechanisms of information seeking.** Neuron. 2024.
   - Supports subjective value of information as part of information-seeking decisions.
   - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2024.04.008

## Emotion / transmission

7. Berger J, Milkman KL. **What Makes Online Content Viral?** Journal of Marketing Research. 2012.
   - Field + experimental evidence that arousal contributes to social transmission.
   - https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.10.0353

8. Tellis GJ, MacInnis DJ, Tirunillai S, Zhang Y. **What Drives Virality (Sharing) of Online Digital Content?** Journal of Marketing. 2019.
   - Multi-platform field evidence on emotion, information, and sharing.
   - https://doi.org/10.1177/0022242919841034

## Identity / influence

9. Spears R. **Social Influence and Group Identity.** Annual Review of Psychology. 2021.
   - Reviews identity-based explanations of social influence and normative processes.
   - https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-070620-111818

## Trust / influencer effects

10. Han J et al. **Meta-analysis of social media influencer impact: Key antecedents and theoretical foundations.** Psychology & Marketing. 2024.
    - 250 effect sizes across 53 studies; strong role for credibility/trustworthiness/expertise, with contextual heterogeneity and some publication-bias concerns.
    - https://doi.org/10.1002/mar.21927

11. Kim DY, Kim HY. **Trust me, trust me not: A nuanced view of influencer marketing on social media.** Journal of Business Research. 2021.
    - Trust as mediator linking source characteristics to loyalty and marketing outcomes.
    - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2021.05.024

## Connection / parasocial engagement

12. **Unrequited love? A mixed-methods study of parasocial engagement with social media influencers.** International Journal of Information Management. 2024.
    - Includes meta-analysis of 117 studies / 47,647 respondents and a qualitative component.
    - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijinfomgt.2024.102798

13. **Consumers' parasocial interactions and relationships with online influencers: a systematic review.** Journal of Consumer Marketing. 2026.
    - Systematic review of 76 peer-reviewed articles.
    - https://doi.org/10.1108/JCM-02-2024-6611

## Reinforcement / habit

14. Wood W, Rünger D. **Psychology of Habit.** Annual Review of Psychology. 2016.
    - Habits as repeated responses in recurring contexts; interaction with goal-directed behavior.
    - https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-122414-033417

15. Anderson BA. **The attention habit: how reward learning shapes attentional selection.** Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences. 2016.
    - Integrates reward learning and attentional selection.
    - PMID: 26595376

## Evidence note

The repository intentionally stores limitations alongside supportive findings. A theory is not promoted to "established" merely because it is popular in marketing practice.
