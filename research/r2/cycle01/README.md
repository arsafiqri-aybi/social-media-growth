# R2 Mechanism Decomposition — Cycle 01

Status: **BREADTH-FIRST DECOMPOSITION COMPLETE; NO L2 NEURON IS FINAL YET**

## What changed

The 45 audited L1 modules now have formal module specifications.

From those boundaries, this cycle derived **116 L2 neuron candidates** across all eight L0 families.

This is not a count target. The candidates exist only where the current R0/R1 evidence supports a useful separable construct/mechanism.

## Distribution

- attention: 14
- curiosity: 13
- valuation_emotion: 19
- identity: 12
- trust: 14
- connection: 12
- social_transmission: 15
- reinforcement_habit: 17

## Legacy migration

All **29 v0.2 sub-neurons** now have an explicit migration disposition:
retain/refine, split, move, or move to connector.

Nothing is silently redefined.

Important migrations:
- homophily leaves Identity and becomes a cross-core similarity connector;
- generic group identity is split into more precise identification components;
- parasocial closeness is split into PSI, PSR, and closeness;
- sharing action is separated from willingness/intention;
- trustworthiness is decomposed from integrity/benevolence;
- reward learning is decomposed from habit mechanisms.

## Connector layer

Eight first-pass context/connectors were created, including:
perceived similarity, relationship reciprocity, communication medium, audience composition, cultural meaning system, platform amplification, relationship history, and active goal state.

These are not psychological neurons unless later evidence shows they should become constructs in their own right.

## Scientific status

The L2 set is **candidate**, not accepted.

Current evidence is mostly landscape-level. R2 Cycle 02 must perform module/neuron-specific evidence audits, especially for constructs marked `candidate_needs_module_specific_evidence`.

A neuron will be promoted only if:
- definition/exclusion boundaries survive overlap audit;
- at least one direct evidence path exists;
- measurement can be specified;
- contradictory/null evidence has been checked;
- cross-core duplication is resolved.
