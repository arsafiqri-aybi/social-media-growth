## Important corrections retained

- Distractor suppression is a mechanism, not merely absence of capture.
- Active information avoidance is not simply low curiosity.
- Approach/avoidance is not a deterministic translation of affective valence.
- Identity threat requires identity-relevant harm appraisal.
- Epistemic vigilance is selective evaluation, not generalized distrust.
- PSI and PSR have different temporal/construct meanings and measures.
- Sharing motives cannot be inferred from sharing action.
- Repetition and stable context contribute to habit formation but are not habit itself.
- Context disruption can weaken habitual control but does not guarantee change.
- No universal "X days creates a habit" rule is permitted.
