## Important non-equivalences

- early capture ≠ sustained attention;
- information gain ≠ epistemic value;
- observed learning improvement ≠ learning-progress signal;
- appraisal dimension ≠ emotion label;
- group prototypicality ≠ homophily;
- authenticity ≠ trustworthiness ≠ truth;
- familiarity ≠ parasocial intimacy;
- privacy risk ≠ reputational risk;
- transmission motive ≠ observed share;
- model-based/model-free arbitration is **not** assumed to be the only architecture of habit-goal control.
