# Global Architecture Falsification Audit v0.1

Date: 2026-10-05  
Result: **PASS — PROVISIONAL LOCK ALLOWED**

## Objective

Attempt to disconfirm the candidate Social Media Growth architecture rather than merely accumulate supporting material.

Candidate:
- Governance
- HPC foundation
- Audience & Value
- Communication & Content
- Distribution & Discovery
- Relationship & Retention
- Learning & Adaptation
- cross-cutting Context / Environment

## Tests

| Candidate change | Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| HPC alone can model full growth | Fails coverage | Reject |
| Brand must be root core | High overlap with communication/relationship | Reject |
| Algorithm must be root core | Platform-specific subset of distribution | Reject |

| Learning & Adaptation can be absorbed into measurement | Too narrow; experimentation/update require separate reasoning functions | Keep |
| Audience & Value should be split | Strong coupling at current abstraction; value is audience-relative | Keep combined provisionally |

## Strongest unresolved challenge

**Audience & Value** is the most plausible future split.

Why:
- audience representation is epistemic/modeling work;
- value creation/value proposition can be treated as a distinct strategic problem.

Why it remains combined:
- value is not meaningful without reference to a target human/context;
- splitting now increases duplicate identity/need/relevance objects;
- current execution benefits more from a tight audience ↔ value hypothesis unit.

Trigger for re-plan:
If subsystem design shows materially different evidence, measurement, and reasoning pipelines that cannot share one core cleanly.

## Relationship & Retention defense

This core remains because:
- relationship states accumulate across multiple content exposures;
- credibility, parasocial/relational mechanisms, community participation, loyalty, and return behavior are not reducible to one content artifact;
- retention can fail even when individual content communication succeeds;
- the literature treats engagement/relationship phenomena as a substantial research family.

Boundary:
Psychological Trust, Connection, Identity, and Reinforcement remain in HPC; Relationship & Retention owns account-level longitudinal composition, not duplicate latent psych constructs.

## Learning & Adaptation defense

This core remains because:
- platform metrics require validity interpretation;
- observational outcomes have competing explanations;
- experimentation requires predictions and falsification;
- account-local evidence must not rewrite scientific evidence;
- a strategy that cannot update from outcomes is not an intelligence system.

## External validity warning

Much published literature is:
- marketing/brand-centric;
- platform-specific;
- observational;
- context-sensitive.

Therefore the global architecture is not claimed as experimentally proven taxonomy. It is a defensible engineering ontology synthesized from recurring research domains and operational necessities.

## Re-plan triggers

Re-enter Scale if:
- a major mechanism cannot be placed without distortion;
- two operational cores repeatedly duplicate the same object classes;
- a new domain has irreducible failure modes across account types;
- benchmarks fail because the current topology hides a necessary distinction;
- measurement cannot distinguish major alternative explanations;
- new evidence materially contradicts a root boundary.
