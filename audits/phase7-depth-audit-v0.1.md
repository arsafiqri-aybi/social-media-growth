# Phase 7 Targeted Depth Audit v0.1

Date: 2026-10-05  
Result: **PASS — MINIMUM HIGH-CENTRALITY DEPTH ACHIEVED**

## Gate questions

### Did Phase 7 deepen all five high-centrality pathways?
PASS.

### Did it avoid arbitrary neuron-count expansion?
PASS.
Reasoning units were created only where separation affects diagnosis, measurement, or intervention.

### Are HPC latent constructs duplicated?
PASS with boundary warnings.
Relationship package uses account-level longitudinal states and hypotheses; Trust, Connection, Identity, Reinforcement/Habit remain HPC constructs.

### Is platform opportunity separated from psychology?
PASS.
Eligibility, ranking, exposure and personalization are system-side objects; attention/value/satisfaction remain separate.

### Are content metrics decomposed?
PASS.
Appeal/selection, continuation and satisfaction are distinct; satisfaction is not inferred from completion alone.

### Is local experimentation causally disciplined?
PASS.
Intervention identifiability is explicit, including targeting/ranking confounds in online platform studies.

### Is segmentation treated cautiously?
PASS.
Clusters/segments require domain/decision validity rather than mathematical separation alone.

### Are scientific/runtime weights introduced?
PASS.
None introduced.

## Residual gaps

- Relevance-fit evidence currently includes advertising-specific work; transport to organic creator contexts remains bounded.
- Familiarity/expectation relationship mechanisms require stronger longitudinal claim-level evidence.
- Platform-specific adapters for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and others are not yet implemented.
- No machine-readable global observation/proxy schema yet.
- No global integrated reasoning runtime yet.

## Decision

Phase 7 v0.1 is sufficient to unlock **Phase 8 — Global Measurement Integration**.

It does not claim scientific saturation.
