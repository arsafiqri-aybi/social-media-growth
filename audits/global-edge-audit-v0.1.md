## Important decisions

### Exposure is not attention
Distribution may create exposure opportunity, but the global engine must not infer HPC Attention from an impression.

### Share is not transmission motive
A logged share is an observation/action. The psychological motive belongs to HPC Social Transmission and may have multiple explanations.

### Return is not habit
Relationship/Retention may ingest returning-viewer observations, but HPC Habit/Reinforcement may only be hypothesized under the measurement rules already established by HPC v1/v2.

### Engagement is not trust
Likes/comments/watch time can enter as observations but cannot auto-activate Trust.

### Platform personalization is a system relation
Official platform documentation supports using interaction/history as ranking/personalization inputs. This is represented as a system edge, not a claim that the platform directly changes a psychological construct.

### Learning edges are account-local control edges
An observed outcome may change operational hypotheses and tactics. It cannot directly change the scientific status of an HPC/global scientific edge.

## Provisional scientific edges

The following interface classes require later claim-level evidence:
- content property → specific HPC attention/curiosity mechanisms;
- source/message cue → specific trust/valuation mechanisms;
- repeated encounter → specific relationship state;
- relationship history → future response;
- social-transmission motive → diffusion behavior under context;
- community participation → specific retention/loyalty mechanism;
- network state → transmission/distribution mechanism;
- portfolio consistency → familiarity/expectation relationship.

Their provisional status is a feature, not a failed gate.
